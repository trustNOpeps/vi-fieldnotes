How much room do I need?

On your PS5, open Settings → Storage and enter free space on the storage location where you plan to install the game. We can't read your console.

Reference: 212 GB available recommended by PlayStation Indonesia (checked 14 Nov 2026). May change; final install size and patch space are not independently verified here.

SAVE BEFORE SPENDING

Know the difference.

PS5 INTERNAL / M.2 SSD

Play PS5 games directly

Compatible M.2 SSD storage can hold and run PS5 games. Check Sony's specifications before buying an expansion.

USB EXTENDED STORAGE

Store, then move back

External USB can store PS5 game files, but PS5 games must be copied to console or M.2 SSD storage before play.
