OUR EDITORIAL STANDARD

Facts are better
than hype.

VI Fieldnotes is a small independent experiment in publishing high-utility, sourced, accessible information.

UNOFFICIAL • INDEPENDENT

How we write

For release dates, versions, platforms, purchase instructions and known characters, we cite Rockstar or platform-holder documentation. Every guide separates confirmed statements from unresolved questions and carries a manual review date. We do not describe untested game mechanics as real experiences.

Our relationship to AI

AI-assisted drafting and website development were used to create this independent resource. This is not a substitute for evidence. Information still needs editorial source checks, corrections and human responsibility; a review date is not an automatic live update.

Conflict of interest

As of the initial publication there are no sponsor relationships, commissions or affiliate accounts connected to this site. Publisher/store links are ordinary links. Future paid placements, if any, must be clearly disclosed near the content they support.

Copyright and affiliation

VI Fieldnotes is not affiliated with, sponsored by, endorsed by or authorized by Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive. Grand Theft Auto and GTA VI are marks of their respective owners. The artwork on this website is an independently created abstract coastal scene; we avoid using official game artwork in promotional commercial placements.

How to verify claims

Use Rockstar’s game page ↗, its pre-load FAQ ↗, editions guide ↗, and regional times ↗ whenever details may change.

Privacy

This static site requires no account and does not collect emails, names or personal profiles. The launch checklist uses local browser storage only. No analytics script is bundled by default. If analytics is added later, the privacy disclosure must be updated accordingly.
