BUSINESS TRANSPARENCY

No hidden deals.

As an early-stage independent project, VI Fieldnotes does not currently operate a paid sponsorship program, an affiliate program or a newsletter sign-up.

What happens next?

We will first evaluate whether readers find our source-linked guides useful. Any future affiliate program requires an approved account, valid product eligibility and a visible commercial disclosure. We will not publish fictional partner logos, rates, sponsor claims or forms that silently discard your message.

For current launch and purchase information, visit the guide library ↗.

What can support this project?

Potential future income streams include approved affiliate links to appropriate gaming accessories, contextual ads after publisher approval, and clearly identified sponsors. None is an active VI Fieldnotes commercial relationship as of 14 November 2026. Ordinary links do not earn commissions for this project.

Before any affiliate link goes live

We will apply to the program under our real identity, register this website as an approved publishing channel where required, confirm the exact eligible product and commission conditions, label the placement as an advertisement or affiliate link near the recommendation, and track actual clicks separately from validated orders and paid commissions. We will not promote counterfeit, account-sharing or unverified software offers.

Editorial independence

Purchases never become a prerequisite for accessing guides. A compensated link would not change the factual advice or source hierarchy. There are no verified sponsor testimonials or partner badges to display today.
