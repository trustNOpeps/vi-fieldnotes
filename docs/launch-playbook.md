# VI Fieldnotes — launch operating playbook

Last reviewed: 14 November 2026. Publisher: VI Fieldnotes (independent resource).

## Mission and editorial promise
Give players verifiable, easy-to-use answers and tools without fictitious gameplay tips or misleading purchase claims. Each factual article includes a dated review stamp, primary source URLs, and a clear distinction between confirmed information and unresolved questions. We do not suggest that AI-generated summaries equal gameplay experience.

## Source priority
1. [Rockstar game page](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI) and official support articles.
2. First-party platform support: [PlayStation Indonesia GTA VI](https://www.playstation.com/en-id/support/games/gta-vi-support/) and [PlayStation storage help](https://www.playstation.com/en-id/support/hardware/ps5-manage-storage-space/).
3. Official platform blog/newsroom for dated announcements. Fan reports, unverified leaks, screenshots without provenance, and third-party predictions do not support definitive claims.

## Pre-release factual baseline (14 November)
- Rockstar lists 19 November 2026 console launch at midnight local time; verify again at launch.
- Digital pre-load began 10 November 2026 with timing dependent on platform.
- PlayStation Indonesia advises 212 GB available space. Treat it as official preparation advice, not a universal installed file size.
- Physical game box is code-in-box rather than disc; match account/store region for code redemption.
- No announced PC date on the Rockstar game page as of this review.

## Publishing rules
- Source at least one directly relevant primary reference for every hard factual claim.
- Headlines must say what the page answers; do not artificially promise a game mechanic, secret map, exploits, or guaranteed performance.
- A new official detail means an editorial review, a new updated date, and an update log entry; never silently auto-change the review date.
- Do not use Rockstar copyrighted promotional artwork or marks to impersonate an official project. State independence prominently.
- Original visual material only. For AI use, maintain disclosure and human fact checks.
- Use plain descriptions and specific helpful steps. Preserve source context rather than copying the source.

## 30-day operational schedule
- Nov 14–18: audit links, indexing and mobile UX; produce launch time, storage, region/edition, safety coverage; verify conversions and GA4 consent.
- Nov 19–23: verify publisher's actual launch status, then review and update all pre-launch articles; only publish gameplay observations after real evidence exists.
- Nov 24–30: share accurate explainers on appropriate communities with non-spam distribution. Track website page views and search clicks.
- Dec 1–7: compare page-level impressions, genuine sessions, engagement, and outbound clicks; identify what problems people still have.
- Dec 8–14: decide whether to scale. Publish transparent metrics with distinctions between estimates and actual income.

## Monetization readiness gate
No paid product endorsements until publisher has been accepted into the relevant affiliate program and the program permits the exact site/channel and placement. Use verified product destinations, no cloaking, no fabricated discount codes, no fake personal reviews. Clearly mark any compensated links near the link and maintain a separate disclosure page. AdSense is an application requiring approval, not an automatic source of income.

## Decision scorecard
- Publishing: all core pages load on mobile and desktop, no console errors, no broken internal links; relevant facts sourced.
- Discovery: Search Console indexing and search impressions measured; no guarantee of rankings.
- Measurement: GA4 page_view requested after consent; actual GA4 receipt separately verified in the dashboard. No analytics before consent.
- Business: clicks to eligible affiliate placements, valid orders and paid commissions measured separately. Gross commissions are not profit; subtract costs and taxes where applicable.

## Escalation
If the official release date changes, update homepage, guide index, timeline, FAQ, checklist and sitemap in the same publication batch. If platform support or the official 212 GB guidance changes, revise the relevant pages before sharing.
