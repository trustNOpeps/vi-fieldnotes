## Production checklist (per post)
1. Reopen primary source and verify date, platform and region.
2. Export original visuals; add high-contrast captions and check safe areas in TikTok/IG/Shorts.
3. No unlicensed Rockstar footage, audio or trademark-like branding. No fake reviews, fake earnings or unfounded first-person gameplay stories.
4. Upload to a **publisher-controlled** account, adjust framing to its audience; comply with community rules, disclose site ownership where relevant.
5. Share a platform-appropriate UTM link; check that it resolves without errors and canonical points to the clean page.
6. In GA4 (for consented traffic), measure sessions and select_content, and in Search Console measure indexed pages/impressions/clicks. Do not infer sales from visits.
7. Record actual published post URL, date, genuine views and clicks; do not report placeholder targets as achievements.
