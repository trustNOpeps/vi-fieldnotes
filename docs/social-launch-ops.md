Updated: **15 November 2026 (Asia/Makassar / WITA)**

## Brand and goals
**Brand:** VI Fieldnotes. Independent editorial guide to GTA VI; not affiliated with Rockstar Games.
**Public landing page:** https://vi-fieldnotes.pages.dev/
**Website content:** 15 guides, PS5 storage checker, ten-step launch checklist. Content is dated; do not claim actual game launch before verified.
**Primary goal:** source-backed audience trust and qualified visits to the website. **No revenue, followers, conversion or reach is presumed.**

Brand voice: plain English for global reach; short Indonesian alternate captions for local audiences. No misinformation, leaked/unlicensed clips, unauthorized GTA artwork, fake views or fake giveaways. Acknowledge AI-assisted production honestly when relevant. For short videos, 9:16 portrait at 1080x1920, keep text legible within UI safe areas; original vector backdrop and self-created graphics only.

- X: `?utm_source=x&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=vifieldnotes_launch`
- IG: `?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=vifieldnotes_launch`

- YouTube: `?utm_source=youtube&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=vifieldnotes_launch`

Append these to a matching Cloudflare URL. GA4 on the site is consent-first: visitors who decline are **not counted by GA4**, so clicks and sessions need not match. Record actual post URLs, impressions, watch time and clicks as observed; never estimate them as fact. Avoid spam and excessive repeated hashtags.
