Status at 14 November 2026: VI Fieldnotes has **no verified active affiliate account, sponsorship, display-ad approval or paid commissions**. The live site does not claim income.

## Priority
1. Build real useful organic visits and source-backed launch guides; collect no personal information unless necessary.
2. Apply to a legitimate affiliate program using the publisher's real identity, ensure the exact website is an approved promotional channel, and check product/channel restrictions.
3. Only after acceptance: add clearly labelled affiliate links to suitable accessory guides, confirm destination and eligibility. Keep neutral non-commission sources.
4. Compare actual program orders and cleared commissions with click events and site costs. Never assume that a click paid a commission.
5. Consider display ads only once site content and policies are suitable and Google (or another network) grants approval; do not pre-load ads or invent earnings.

## Decision gate at 30 days
- Continue if useful pages show repeat demand, accurate content can be maintained, and operating costs are defensible.
- Pivot if qualified users want a narrower topic, such as PS5 buyer prep, rather than broad gaming headlines.
- Stop or archive commercial plans if no measurable qualified audience emerges and the maintenance effort exceeds value.
- Never promote improbable earnings estimates as actual results.
