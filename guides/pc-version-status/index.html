Is GTA VI coming to PC?

What is announced versus what fans expect.

Last reviewed: 14 Nov 2026Independent editorial
THE SHORT ANSWER

No PC launch date is published alongside the current console launch plans on the official GTA VI page.

What is confirmed

Rockstar’s published GTA VI launch platforms are PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. That information is the basis of our current launch guides.

What we cannot claim

A future PC release is possible, but possibility is not a committed product, date, launcher, or hardware requirement. This guide does not invent specifications or availability windows.

How to follow updates

Watch Rockstar Games’ official game page and verified publisher news. Treat retailer placeholders and viral social posts as unconfirmed until corroborated by the publisher.

WHAT'S CONFIRMED

No specific PC release date is announced on Rockstar’s cited game page.

WHAT STILL NEEDS CONFIRMATION

PC hardware requirements and release window remain unconfirmed.
