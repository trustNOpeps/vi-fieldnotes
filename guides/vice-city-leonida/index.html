What we know about the setting

Separate Rockstar’s official world introduction from fan speculation.

Last reviewed: 14 Nov 2026Independent editorial
THE SHORT ANSWER

The official material places GTA VI in Vice City and the fictional state of Leonida.

The confirmed backdrop

Rockstar’s official game page introduces Vice City and Leonida as the setting. The environment is part of the announced creative premise, not an invitation to assert every rumored district or collectible location.

What previews can show

Official promotional text and trailers are legitimate starting points for describing the atmosphere, landscape and cultural references. They do not prove where hidden items or missions will be located.

How this guide will evolve

Once reliable first-hand footage and official documents are available, we can add independently checked navigation tips. Until then, we will not publish invented location guides for search traffic.

WHAT'S CONFIRMED

Vice City and Leonida are official setting names.

WHAT STILL NEEDS CONFIRMATION

Detailed collectible maps and exact gameplay routes are not verified before launch.
