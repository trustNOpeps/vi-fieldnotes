Where can you play GTA VI at launch?

A straightforward check of officially announced platforms, without inventing a PC release date.

Last reviewed: 19 Nov 2026Independent editorial
THE SHORT ANSWER

Rockstar lists PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S as launch platforms. No official PC date is provided on its current GTA VI page.

Confirmed console platforms

The published launch lineup consists of PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. The practical buying decision begins with the console you already own, a compatible storefront account, and the game edition you want.

What about older consoles?

Rockstar announced Xbox Cloud Gaming for Xbox One and Series X|S on November 10, using Series S graphics settings. Xbox One is not a native release platform. Streaming depends on connectivity and regional service eligibility; PS4 support has not been announced.

And PC?

Do not treat rumors about PC timing as announced facts. Monitor Rockstar’s official site for any additional platform confirmation before making purchase or hardware plans.

WHAT'S CONFIRMED

PS5 and Xbox Series X|S are published launch platforms.

WHAT STILL NEEDS CONFIRMATION

No PC launch date is confirmed by the publisher on the cited page.
