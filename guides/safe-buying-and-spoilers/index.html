Safety / VERIFIED PRE-LAUNCH GUIDE

Avoid scams, fake codes and unwanted spoilers

A short checklist for safer launch-week decisions.

Last reviewed: 14 Nov 2026Independent editorial
THE SHORT ANSWER

Use official stores and confirmed publisher links. Do not trust “free full download” promotions.

Purchase through known channels

Check retailer legitimacy, exact platform, game edition, supported country and code region. Take a screenshot of the store terms and keep your proof of purchase.

Recognize warning signs

An unofficial installer, cracked early access offer, or activation request asking for your account password is a red flag. Never provide platform credentials to unlock a promotion.

Control spoiler exposure

Mute relevant keywords and avoid unsolicited livestream highlights if you want to preserve story surprises. We will clearly mark any post-launch spoiler-heavy guides when they are added.

WHAT'S CONFIRMED

Rockstar provides preorder and purchase support.

WHAT STILL NEEDS CONFIRMATION

Claims about leaked content or unofficial unlocking are not verified.
