November 19, 2026: the essential timeline

Start with the date Rockstar actually publishes, then confirm your own region.

Last reviewed: 14 Nov 2026Independent editorial
THE SHORT ANSWER

November 19, 2026 is the announced console launch date. Regional release guidance is more useful than a viral countdown.

What is officially scheduled?

Rockstar currently lists November 19, 2026 as the release date for Grand Theft Auto VI on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. This site treats it as the announced schedule, not a guarantee against future changes.

Which clock matters?

Rockstar publishes platform and region-specific release timings. Digital storefronts may display a local countdown; check your account region and the publisher’s regional guidance rather than assuming a single global hour.

What should I do now?

Check the console platform, your purchase edition, storage availability, and whether any pre-load is available in your region. Prefer the official store when checking live account entitlements.

WHAT'S CONFIRMED

The official announced launch is November 19, 2026.

WHAT STILL NEEDS CONFIRMATION

Gameplay details and unofficial release-time screenshots are not independently verified.
