Should you upgrade your console?

Do not mistake platform marketing for an independently measured benchmark.

Last reviewed: 14 Nov 2026Independent editorial
THE SHORT ANSWER

PlayStation published PS5 Pro enhancements in its November 10 announcement; only official statements are included here.

What is the question?

Both PlayStation 5 and PlayStation 5 Pro belong to the published PS5 platform family. The more useful question is whether specific enhanced visuals are worth paying for a console upgrade.

Avoid unverified frame-rate claims

The PlayStation launch blog discusses enhancements including PSSR and ray-traced shadows. Do not convert that into a claimed universal frame rate, locked resolution or loading time without controlled evidence.

How to decide

If you already own a compatible PS5, evaluate the experience and independently tested comparisons before spending more. Console availability and prices vary across regions.

WHAT'S CONFIRMED

PlayStation has published PS5 Pro enhancement information.

WHAT STILL NEEDS CONFIRMATION
