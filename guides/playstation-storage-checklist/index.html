Prepare storage for the download

Check available space before pre-load and updates.

Last reviewed: 14 Nov 2026Independent editorial
THE SHORT ANSWER

The PlayStation US GTA VI page lists a substantial storage requirement; verify the number shown for your region and version.

Check your console first

Go to console settings and confirm available storage. PlayStation’s US store page has listed 212 GB; actual download, installation and patch sizes can change. Leave extra margin rather than clearing exactly that amount.

Understand the purchase format

A physical code-in-box version may still require a large download. A digital pre-load also needs free space, a stable connection and the correct store account.

Do not delete blindly

Back up valuable game saves and remove unwanted installations only after checking what they contain. If storage is insufficient, follow the console manufacturer’s official storage expansion instructions.

WHAT'S CONFIRMED

Official PlayStation store provides a storage listing.

WHAT STILL NEEDS CONFIRMATION

The final installed size and future patch size can change.
